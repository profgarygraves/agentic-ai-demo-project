SOP Alignment

Demonstration Project lifecycle responsibilities.

01

Initiation and Engagement | Planning

Confirm the project structure.

Complete the Project Development Template and RACI document, hold kickoff, create the SharePoint project folder, and confirm access for accountable and responsible stakeholders.

02

Activation and Integration | Onboarding

Place the project on record and establish metrics.

Prepare for BOG presentation or July BOG report inclusion, complete Metrics Phase I within three weeks, prepare Metrics Phase II, and connect with the CCCCO Data Lead and Data Liaison.

03

Progress Tracking | Reporting

Report twice per quarter.

Submit midterm and quarterly reports covering highlights, activities, support needs, RACI, V2030 outcomes, college participants, artifacts, metrics, and next steps.

04

Reflection and Learning | Closeout

Decide whether to scale, transition, or close.

Complete the Closeout Form, schedule closeout review, present findings to the CO executive team, archive final deliverables, and summarize lessons learned.

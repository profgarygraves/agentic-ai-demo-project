Agentic AI Demo Project

A research-first demonstration project exploring how agentic AI can help colleges move from reactive systems of record to proactive systems of action for student support, enrollment, teaching, and institutional operations.

Problem of Practice

Student support is too reactive, fragmented, and labor-intensive.

The project responds to a persistent gap across community colleges: digital systems such as CRM, SIS, and LMS platforms hold important student data, but they often remain passive systems of record. Students must initiate help, staff must manually follow up, and support often arrives after the moment of need.

Agentic AI creates a path toward systems that can reason, plan, and take approved action across tools. The demonstration project will test whether that shift can improve enrollment completion, student engagement, staff capacity, advising support, and course development workflows while maintaining strong human oversight.
